## Problem Statement

### RM-201 Indoor Air Quality Control

The objective is to model a single-zone demand-controlled outdoor-air system representing a room, outdoor-air source, exhaust path, occupant CO2 generation, room CO2 measurement, and a feedback controller for ventilation.

The system requirements include maintaining room CO2 below 1000 ppm during the approved occupied schedule, supporting peak occupancy of at least 12 persons, and representing CO2 generation, airflow, and control feedback with appropriate engineering units and verification evidence.
