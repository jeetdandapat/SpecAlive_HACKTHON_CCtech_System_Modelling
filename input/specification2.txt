1. Purpose and Scope
The package controls a demonstration liquid-transfer process consisting of two tanks, three actuated
isolation valves, two level measurements and three operator pushbuttons. The controller is expected to
execute the normal sequence automatically once started and to support pause/resume and controlled
shutdown commands.

3. Functional Requirements
URS-F-001 - START shall initiate automatic operation when the system is in its initial condition.
URS-F-002 - Normal operation shall fill Tank 1 by opening V1.
URS-F-003 - V1 shall close when Tank 1 reaches the specified high-level limit.
URS-F-004 - After a 10 s waiting period, V2 shall open and transfer liquid from Tank 1 to Tank 2.
URS-F-005 - V2 shall close when Tank 1 reaches its minimum operating level.
URS-F-006 - After a 10 s waiting period, V3 shall open and drain Tank 2.
URS-F-007 - V3 shall close when Tank 2 reaches its minimum operating level.
URS-F-008 - The automatic process shall continue cyclically unless stopped or shut down.
URS-M-001 - STOP shall close all three valves and suspend automatic operation until another operator command is
received.
URS-M-002 - START after STOP shall continue the process operation rather than force a new empty-cycle
initialization.
URS-M-003 - SHUT shall empty both tanks by opening V2 and V3 and shall inhibit V1.
URS-M-004 - When shutdown emptying is achieved, V2 and V3 shall close and the controller shall return to the start
configuration.

5. Interfaces
Level measurements LT-101 and LT-102 shall be available to PLC-101. START, STOP and SHUT are
momentary commands. Valve commands are binary open/closed outputs. The supplier shall provide
actuators that fail closed on loss of power.
6. Verification Intent
Sequence operation, stop/resume behavior and controlled shutdown shall be demonstrated in simulation.
The exact demonstration command schedule will be issued in the validation procedure.
