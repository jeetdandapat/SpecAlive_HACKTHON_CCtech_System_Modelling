RM-201 Indoor Air Quality Control - Owner
Requirements
Document URS-IAQ-001 - Revision A - 2026-01-08
1. Scope
This specification defines the owner requirements for the RM-201 single-zone demand-controlled outdoor-air
demonstration. The system shall represent a room, an outdoor-air source, an exhaust boundary, occupant
CO2 generation, a room CO2 measurement, and a feedback controller that adjusts ventilation.
2. Functional requirements
URS-IAQ-001 - The room shall be represented as a single well-mixed air volume of 100 m3.
URS-IAQ-002 - Outdoor air shall be supplied through a controlled airflow path and room air shall leave through an
exhaust path.
URS-IAQ-003 - The ventilation controller shall modulate fresh-air flow using measured room CO2 as feedback.
URS-IAQ-004 - During the approved occupied schedule, room CO2 shall not exceed 1000 ppm.
URS-IAQ-005 - The reference outdoor-air CO2 concentration shall be 300 ppm.
URS-IAQ-006 - CO2 generation shall be proportional to the scheduled number of occupants.
URS-IAQ-007 - The design occupant CO2 generation rate shall be 8.18E-6 kg/s per person.
URS-IAQ-008 - The released demonstration shall support a peak occupancy of at least 12 persons. Final operations
schedule to be issued separately.
3. Control and verification expectations
URS-IAQ-009 - A numerical simulation shall demonstrate the CO2 feedback behavior over a representative daily
occupancy schedule.
URS-IAQ-010 - Concentration and airflow quantities shall be represented with explicit engineering units or
unambiguous normalization definitions.
URS-IAQ-011 - The model shall distinguish physical outdoor-air flow direction from any implementation-specific
source sign convention.
URS-IAQ-012 - Verification evidence shall record occupant count, room CO2, outdoor CO2, commanded ventilation,
and physical ventilation flow.
Revision note. Rev A is the baseline owner document. Detailed controls tuning, the final occupancy schedule, and resolution of
ambiguous wording may be established by later approved engineering records.
